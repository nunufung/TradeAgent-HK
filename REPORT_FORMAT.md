# Daily research report format

PDF and Telegram summaries use the same rule-gated decision. Newly generated reports apply this format; previously generated delivery artifacts are not retroactively changed.

1. Decision summary: today's action, contract availability and the primary reason.
2. Option assessment: at most one research candidate, explicitly separated from an approved entry. Preserve quote timestamp, contract, expiry, Greeks, liquidity and margin information.
3. Stock research: concise extracts of the actual technical, sentiment, news and fundamental reports, alongside the integrated research conclusion. Use Chinese rating labels with the original rating retained.
4. Execution plan: entry and cancellation conditions, take-profit, loss management, expiry management and roll requirements.
5. Data notes: research method, outstanding verification and account-information limitations. Detailed original research remains in the existing research artifacts; individual-stock PDFs place their research material after the decision brief.

The brief uses deterministic extracts, not a new model opinion. Missing information remains a data gap, rather than a neutral signal or a verified fact. A stock-level Underweight or Sell rating is not an instruction to open a short call. Screening rules and fail-closed trading decisions remain unchanged.

Telegram puts the option decision before stock research and avoids repeating a second final conclusion. PDF headings stay with their following paragraph; original source links remain clickable in the news section.

For public showcases, use labeled historical or demonstration inputs and review all content for personal information before sharing. Do not present synthetic fixtures as current recommendations.
